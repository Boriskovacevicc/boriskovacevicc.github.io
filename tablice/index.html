How scoring works

Enter each side’s card points. There are 22. With two or four players, the side with the most cards gets 3 more, so the cards add up to 25, or 22 on a tie. Three players have no bonus.

Tabla is an extra point for that player, added on top. It is not part of the 22 or 25. A side that finishes a round on 90 or more (40 in a game to 51) cannot score more tablas after that.

If 22 card points cannot be shared without someone reaching the target, the round is marked Final round and the total is not checked. Finish game saves a partial score and ends the match only when a side has got there.
